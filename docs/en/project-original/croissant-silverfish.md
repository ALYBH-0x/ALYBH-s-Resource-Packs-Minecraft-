# Croissant Silverfish

> Replaces the Silverfish with adorable croissant-themed textures and models, adding a humorous and bakery-inspired twist to your game.

<details>
<summary>Click to expand release pages</summary>

- Modrinth：https://modrinth.com/project/rNKiWRiR
</details>

## Overview

It is a creative Minecraft resource pack that transforms the usual creepy silverfish into playful croissant-shaped creatures.

Instead of the default insect-like appearance, players will encounter flaky, golden pastry-inspired mobs that bring a lighthearted and comedic feel to underground exploration.

This pack is perfect for players who enjoy food-themed aesthetics, meme-style texture packs, or simply want to make their Minecraft world a little more delicious and less terrifying.

## Compatibility

### Supported Versions

Compatible with **Minecraft Java Edition 1.6 and newer**.

In vanilla Minecraft, custom textures and models for the silverfish spawn egg only work in 1.13 and newer.

### Resource Usage

**Vanilla Minecraft:**

- Textures: silverfish, silverfish spawn egg (model)
- Models: silverfish (uses vanilla internal model), silverfish spawn egg

## Trivia

- This pack is an accidental side project created during the development of [Cute Blocky Silverfish](./cute-blocky-silverfish.md):
<details><summary>Spoiler Warning: Hidden content below</summary>

> "This little quirky, strangely unsettling texture creation of mine isn’t actually some carefully planned piece of work at all. In all honesty, it is merely one of those weird, unintentionally eerie, cursed-looking accidental side outputs, the kind of unintended leftover derivative result that came straight from my previously released texture pack project known as Cute Blocky Silverfish. That is to say, if you take a close look at its odd, misshapen visual form, you can clearly tell it bears a striking, almost unsettling resemblance to wriggling maggots, which is exactly why this strangely cursed, maggot-resembling little mob texture was unused in that pack. But I suppose that someone would like it so released it individually."
</details>
