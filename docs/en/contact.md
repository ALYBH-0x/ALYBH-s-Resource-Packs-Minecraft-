# Contact

This page lists available contact methods, their applicable scenarios, and notes for communication.

[Return to Home](./home.md)

## Contact Channels

### GitHub Issues

Link: https://github.com/ALYBH-0x/ALYBH-s-Resource-Packs-Minecraft-/issues

**Applicable for:** Bug reports, documentation corrections, and other matters requiring changes or fixes from the repository and project maintainers.

> Please search for duplicate Issues before submitting. Add information to the existing thread for duplicates. Maintainers will respond at their discretion; no guaranteed response time.

### Email

Email：`ALYBH-0x@outlook.com`

**Applicable for:** Formal matters including license inquiries, permission requests, cooperation discussions, and other cases requiring written records, formal workflows and timely replies.

> Emails are usually processed within 7 calendar days. If you receive no reply after this period, please check your spam folder or resend your message. During formal email correspondence within the same time frame, I may follow up to confirm if I do not hear back from you.

### Others

**Social Media Direct Messages:**

Bilibili：https://space.bilibili.com/3707013659888454

**Applicable for:** Short notices and casual private messages.

> This channel is for informal supplementary contact only. Replies are not guaranteed. Matters covered by the channels above are not recommended to be sent via direct message.

## Notes

- Please use the proper channel for your matter first. If you send a message through the correct channel and get no reply after the stated time, review the notes for that channel first. You may send a reminder through another listed channel if necessary.
- When sending inquiries, please include relevant context and details to help with verification and handling.
- Unless it is an emergency, please refrain from using other contact methods not listed on this page, as messages sent through those channels are unlikely to receive a reply.
- Irrelevant advertisements, promotions, or vague messages without clear requests or background will not be replied to.
