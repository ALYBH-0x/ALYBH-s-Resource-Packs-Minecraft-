# Index

This is the index page, listing resource packs and related entries by category.

Click to expand categories for an overview. Click an item title to open its detail page.

It is recommended to obtain all resources through the verified release channels listed inside each corresponding resource pack entry.

Unofficial mirrors or repackaged files from third parties are not recommended, as the integrity and safety of resources distributed via those channels cannot be guaranteed.

[Back to Home](./home.md)

## Original Resource Pack Projects

<details>
<summary>ALYBH‑REconstruction Style</summary>

> A series of resource packs with a personal art style.

- **[ALYBH‑REconstruction](./project-original/alybh-re-construction.md)**
A set of resource packs that keeps vanilla color palettes while redrawing block and item textures. It includes base textures, mob textures, 3D models, emissive textures, 512x parallax textures, shield & banner conversion textures, simplified PVP textures and optional add‑on packs; some can be freely combined.

- **[Cute Blocky Silverfish](./project-original/cute-blocky-silverfish.md)**
Updates the Silverfish with a blockier, 20% cuter design! Furthermore, additional functionality can be realized with the help of game mods.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Vanilla-like Tweaks</summary>

> Resource packs built around Minecraft’s vanilla art style.

- **[3D Vanilla‑like Items](./project-original/3d-vanilla-like-items.md)**
Adds emboss-style vanilla-friendly 3D models for swords, tools, bows, crossbows, arrows and more. Texture layouts are highly compatible with vanilla, making it easy for texture replacement and further edits.

- **[Bow and Crossbow Visual Changes](./project-original/bow-and-crossbow-visual-change.md)**
Swaps the first‑person held visuals of bows and crossbows. It is also split into two standalone subpacks: Horizontal Bow and Vertical Crossbow for flexible combination.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Enchantment Glints</summary>

> Resource packs focused on enchantment glint textures.

- **[ALYBH‑REnchantment Glints](./project-original/alybh-re-enchantment-glint.md)**
A collection of enchantment glint packs with dozens of alternative shimmer effects in different colors, gradients and patterns. No mods required.

- **[ALYBH's CIT Recolored Enchantment Glints](./project-original/alybh-cit-recolored-enchantment-glint.md)**
Requires the CIT‑Enchantment feature from CIT Resewn or OptiFine. Uses CIT to automatically assign different glint colors based on enchantment types, so you can tell enchanted items apart at a glance.

- **[ALYBH's CIT Custom Glints](./project-original/alybh-cit-custom-enchantment-glint.md)**
Requires the CIT‑Enchantment feature from CIT Resewn or OptiFine. Players can freely mix colors and patterns to customize enchant glints by renaming items in an anvil or using item Lore tags.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Misc.</summary>

> Resource packs without a dedicated category for now.

- **[Croissant Silverfish](./project-original/croissant-silverfish.md)**
Croissant‑themed textures and models for silverfish, adding bakery‑style fun and humor to the game.
</details>
